Model your own drill data

Upload collar, survey and assay files — CSV, Excel, Access or a zip of them — and MineModelingPro builds the same 3D model it builds from technical reports: desurveyed holes coloured by grade, an inverse-distance block model on the block size you choose, grade-tonnage and contained metal, on real terrain.

Your data stays private. Projects are visible only to your account until you choose to share one. You can keep adding data to a project over time, or merge your drilling with a project already in the public gallery.

What to upload

TableNeedsAlso understood
Collarshole id, easting/northing (or lat/long)elevation, azimuth, dip, total depth, feet or metres
Assayshole id, from, to (or length), one grade column per elementlong format (element + value), ppm/ppb/%/g/t/oz-t
Surveyshole id, depth, azimuth, dipused for minimum-curvature hole paths

Column names are matched by meaning — HOLEID, Hole_No, BHID, DDH all work. Tell us the EPSG code or UTM zone and your grid is placed on the map; without one the model still builds in its own local grid.
